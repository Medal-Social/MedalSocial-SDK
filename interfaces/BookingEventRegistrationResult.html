    Interface BookingEventRegistrationResult

    Result of bookings.events.register(...).

    payment is null when the registration's service needs no payment. manage_token is SHOW-ONCE, exactly like CreatedBooking.manage_token — persist it here or it is gone. It is present on every fresh registration; an idempotent replay omits it (only its hash is stored, so a replay cannot reproduce the plaintext).
