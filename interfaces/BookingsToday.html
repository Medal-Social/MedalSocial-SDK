    Interface BookingsToday

    The salon's operating summary for ONE local date — Medal's own «I dag» board over the API.

    The date is the WORKSPACE's, not the caller's: omit date_key and the workspace time zone decides which Thursday this is. truncated says a source hit its read cap, so the counts are floors rather than totals.
