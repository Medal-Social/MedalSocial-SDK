    Interface BookingPayment

    One payment attempt on a booking.

    Money is integer øre, and the four aggregates are numbers rather than nulls: "nothing has been captured" is 0, so summing them never needs a null guard. redirect_url is deliberately absent — it is handed over once by BookingPaymentStart and never re-read.
