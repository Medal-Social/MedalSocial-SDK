A site's own word for a grown-up now survives in two more setups. A component's `people.adult` under a provider given a whole built-in pack (`labels={mergeLabels('nb')}`) is no longer replaced by «Person {n}»: the built-in numbered word in an earlier layer is not an opt-in. A numbered word the site chose itself (for example `'Gjest {n}'` in `config.labels`) is now kept when a provider or component renames the grown-up.
