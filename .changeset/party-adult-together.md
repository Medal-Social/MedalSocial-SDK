Config `party.adultTogether` (default `false`): a party with a grown-up in it starts «Samtidig», side by side, and goes back to «Rett etter hverandre» when the grown-up leaves it. It stops deciding once the parent picks a mode or a party slot, and a party with a named stylist stays one after another. A restored draft comes back in the mode it was saved in. New optional `WizardState.partyModeChosen`.
