Config `screens.wideLayouts` (default `false`): the stylist step keeps its row of faces at every width, with the chosen stylist's bio under it, and the time step uses the adaptive layout. On an iPad the days sit beside the times; on a desktop the whole week is a grid of times. Built-in nb/en words for `time.week.none` and `time.week.more`.

Requires `@medalsocial/meda` ^3.8.0.
