    DeliveryQuote: Schemas["DeliveryQuote"]

    A binding Wolt price from delivery.quotes.create().

    • customer_fee: what the store charges its customer (the restaurant's fee policy applied), null when there is none to show.
    • cost: Wolt's price to the restaurant.
    • eta_minutes / delivered_by: when the order reaches the customer if booked now (ISO 8601 UTC) — never a pickup time.
    • valid_until: when the price runs out (ISO 8601 UTC). An order needs at least one more minute of it (422 QUOTE_EXPIRED otherwise).
    • address: the address as Wolt will deliver to it.
